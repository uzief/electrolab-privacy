ElectroLab: Resistor Academy is an educational game developed by DokarTech. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the application.

ElectroLab does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, physical address, or identity information directly to us.

Game progress and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

2. Advertising

ElectroLab uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information related to advertising interaction in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

ElectroLab does not directly request or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number for advertising purposes.

For more information about how Google handles data for advertising, please review Google's privacy and advertising policies.

3. Third-Party Services

ElectroLab uses third-party services to provide advertising functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The third-party service currently used for advertising is:

4. Local Game Data

ElectroLab stores certain game-related information locally on the user's device, including game progress, best scores, and application preferences such as sound, haptic feedback, and theme settings.

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not intended to identify the user.

5. Data Sharing

DokarTech does not sell users' personal information.

Information processed by third-party services, including advertising services, is handled according to the policies and terms of those services.

ElectroLab is an educational game. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us.

Advertising services may have their own policies and requirements regarding children's privacy and advertising.
